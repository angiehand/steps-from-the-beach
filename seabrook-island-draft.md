# Seabrook Island Rentals: What You Need to Know Before You Book

Seabrook Island is one of the quietest, most genuinely natural beaches on the East Coast — no strip malls, no crowds, no noise. But it's also a private, gated community, which means it operates by its own rules. Here's what you actually need to know before you start planning.

---

## Know Before You Go

**Seabrook Island is a private island.** You cannot drive on, walk on, or access the island unless you are:

- A **registered guest** staying in a rental property on the island
- A **property owner** or their invited guest
- A **licensed real estate agent** showing properties with an appointment

There is a staffed security gate at the entrance. If your name isn't on the guest list for your rental, you will be turned away — this applies to day visitors, family drop-ins, and anyone not listed on the reservation. When you book a rental, make sure every person in your group is added to the guest list through your property manager before you arrive.

**No beach day trips.** Unlike most beach towns, you cannot park nearby and walk to the beach. Access is for residents and registered guests only.
