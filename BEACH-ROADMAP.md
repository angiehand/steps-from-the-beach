# Steps from the Beach — Beach Roadmap

Last updated: 2026-08-26

Tracks which beaches are live, next, planned, or still being researched, plus
notes on homepage structure. Update as beaches launch or the list changes.

## Brand principles

- **Curating appeal, not luxury.** A $500/night condo with one great
  ocean-view breakfast nook belongs on the list right alongside a
  $2,000/night house. The bar is: attractive, close to the beach, and has
  something about it that makes you want to stay there — not price point.
  This is what makes the site more useful and more defensible than a
  generic "luxury beach rentals" site.
- **Build the rental finder before adding content.** No restaurant guides,
  packing lists, "10 things to do" posts, etc. for now — that dilutes the
  core product. Beach pages first: Kiawah → Seabrook → next → next. Blog
  content is a good later-stage SEO play (see SEO-TODO.md), not a now one.
- **Scarcity is a feature.** If a beach has 1,000 rentals and only 18 meet
  the bar, show 18. The selectivity itself is the trust signal — "we don't
  list every rental, only the ones we'd stay in ourselves."

## Homepage structure

Group beaches into two sections by state — "South Carolina" and "North
Carolina" — rather than one flat grid. Simple to scan, matches how people
actually plan trips, and sets up naturally for SEO later: if either state's
list grows long, that section can become its own landing page (e.g. "South
Carolina Beach Rentals") linking out to the individual beach pages, adding
internal linking depth the site is currently light on.

A beach's page only goes live once it has a few real hand-picked properties
on it — an empty/placeholder page hurts SEO (thin content) and first
impressions, so "planned" beaches stay off the live site until populated.
