About Steps from the Beach

How We Choose

There are thousands of places to rent at the beach. We're only interested in the ones that feel a little more special.

Why we're picky

Two things matter most when we're choosing a beach rental: how close it is to the sand, and whether someone has put real thought into making it a beautiful place to stay.

Most rental sites let you filter by bedrooms, amenities, and location. We're looking for something a little harder to filter for — the places where the design itself is part of the appeal.

The standard
"Clean and updated" is the floor, not the bar

Every rental worth staying in should be clean, well-maintained, and accurately photographed. That's the starting point, not the reason it makes our list.

We're looking for the places that went further — the ones where someone clearly cared about the details. A thoughtfully furnished sunroom. A kitchen you'd actually want to cook in. Bedrooms with beautiful linens, art, and details that don't feel like an afterthought.

It doesn't have to be the biggest, newest, or most expensive rental. Thoughtful beats expensive every time.

That's the difference between a place to sleep and a place that becomes part of the trip.

We don't list everything

If an island has a thousand rentals and only a handful meet that bar, we'll show you the handful.

We'd rather give you a short list worth considering than a long one you have to sift through yourself. That's the whole point.

What we look for

We check how close each property really is to beach access, rather than relying solely on descriptions like "steps from the beach."

Then we look at the home itself — the design, furnishings, outdoor spaces, views, and details that make it stand out.

And in every home, we call out one Standout Space: the room, view, or corner that caught our attention and made us want to keep looking.

We're not here to critique the homes that don't make the list. We're here to celebrate the ones where someone clearly put extra thought and care into creating a stay that feels special.

One important note

Our picks are based on the property listings, photographs, location information, and other details available to us at the time we feature them. Unless we specifically say otherwise, we haven't personally stayed in or inspected the property.

Listings, furnishings, and beach access can change, so always confirm the details that matter most to you directly with the property or management company before booking.
